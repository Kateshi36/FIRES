WHAT THE APP CODE SAYS (checked in IncidentActionRules, LocationShareRules, IncidentDetailScreen)
 1. "Start response" needs BOTH: status = DISPATCHED  AND  the signed-in responder's uid is the responderId of an
    assignment (LocationShareRules.canStart).
 2. The website cannot dispatch. After Assign the report is still "Verified". So your note is only half right:
    the assigned responder gets "Start response" AFTER someone presses "Mark as dispatched" in the app.
    (Any responder can press it, not only the assigned one.)
 3. Before dispatch, an assigned responder sees no "Start response" and no explanation. After dispatch, a responder
    who is NOT assigned sees the note "Only a responder assigned to this incident can share a live location."
 4. The app has NO notification for "you were assigned". The responder only finds out by opening the report.
 5. Wording problem: the app's Assigned panel says "Assigned by <name>". On the website the saved name is the person
    ASSIGNED, so it will read "Assigned by Ana Cruz" when the admin assigned Ana. In the app the assigner and the
    assignee were always the same person (the rules force it), so "Assigned to <name>" is right for both. One-word
    fix in IncidentDetailScreen.kt (AssignmentRow). Say "fix 5" if you want it.
